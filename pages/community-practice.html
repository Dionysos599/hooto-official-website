(03)COMMUNITY PRACTICE / INCLUSION

公益实践Community Practice

如果美育是给所有青少年的，
那就不该有谁被漏掉。 If arts education is for all young people,
no one should be left out.

团队成员自2017年起持续走进特殊教育学校，通过艺术活动、青年志愿者陪伴与社会融合项目，让表达的机会抵达更广阔的青少年群体。

Since 2017, our team has continued to work with special education schools through art activities, youth volunteer companionship and social inclusion projects, bringing opportunities for expression to a wider group of young people.
