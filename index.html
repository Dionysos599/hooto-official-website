(03) 社会使命Social Mission

从一次相遇，
到长久的陪伴。From one encounter
to lasting companionship.

公益对我们来说从来不是一次突然的到访，而是愿意回来，继续相处。把每一次实践留下的理解与经验，带到下一次相遇中。

For us, public good is never a one-off visit. It means choosing to return, staying connected, and carrying the understanding gained from every practice into the next encounter.

(04) 关于About

换个角度，
看世界。See the world
differently.

“花头精”是吴语里对点子多、爱琢磨的人的称呼。HOOTO于2025年诞生于苏州，由来自艺术、建筑、工程与商业领域的年轻人共同发起。

“Huatoujing” is a Wu dialect term for someone full of ideas and eager to explore them. HOOTO was founded in Suzhou in 2025 by young people from art, architecture, engineering and business.

一个问题，可以变成作品；一张线稿，可以长成角色；一次真实的相遇，自然也可以成为改变的起点。花头精HOOTO从艺术出发，在美育、公益、IP与科技之间寻找新的连接。我们相信，世界不只有一种看法，成长也不只有一种答案。我们不急着给出标准答案，只想和更多人一起，看见那些容易被忽略的可能。

A question can become a work; a sketch can grow into a character; a real encounter can become the beginning of change. Starting from art, HOOTO connects education, public good, IP and technology. We believe there is more than one way to see the world and more than one answer to growth.

有一个好点子？一起把它做出来。Have an idea? Let’s make it real.
